QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know

Why do I need a mapping pack?

Legacy Gujarati fonts often assign different visible letters to different character codes. A pack supplies that font-specific table. This site includes the conversion engine, not proprietary mapping tables.

Will this install or provide the legacy font?

No. Font files are separate from text conversion. Use fonts you are licensed to install, and check that your target app has the right font available when viewing legacy output.

What can I do before importing a pack?

You can paste Gujarati Unicode text and normalize it to NFC. Full legacy conversion becomes available after you load a valid map.
